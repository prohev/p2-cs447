	A dozen years ago I shared my mother-in-law&#8217;s recipe for <a href="https://smittenkitchen.com/2012/01/apple-sharlotka/">apple sharlotka</a> (which family just calls &#8220;apple thing&#8221;), a lightly sweetened apple dessert that&#8217;s as much a thick crêpe as it is a cake. It&#8217;s got a short ingredient list and is the kind of thing you make on a whim. It&#8217;s so rustic and simple, I honestly didn&#8217;t expect it to cause a ruckus &#8212; it&#8217;s not <a href="https://smittenkitchen.com/2018/10/even-more-perfect-apple-pie/">apple pie</a>, <a href="https://smittenkitchen.com/2021/10/big-apple-crumb-cake/">crumb cake</a>, or even my mom&#8217;s <a href="https://smittenkitchen.com/2008/09/moms-apple-cake/">doorstop of an apple cake</a> &#8212;  but you surprised me. It has over 1000 comments and I&#8217;ve seen variations on it all over the internet. So where does this come in? I think of this as Sharlotka 2.0: Fancy Pants Edition. </p>
