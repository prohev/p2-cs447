Subject: Record sea surface temperature jump in 2023–2024 unlikely but not unexpected

                <p>Nature, Published online: 12 March 2025; <a href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08674-z">doi:10.1038/s41586-025-08674-z</a></p>Observations and climate models suggest that the global sea surface temperature jump in 2023–2024 was not unexpected and would have been nearly impossible without anthropogenic warming.
