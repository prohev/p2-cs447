                <p>Nature, Published online: 12 March 2025; <a href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08681-0">doi:10.1038/s41586-025-08681-0</a></p>A Homo aff. erectus individual dated to 1.4 million to 1.1 million years ago found at Sima del Elefante (Sierra de Atapuerca, Spain) does not display the modern-human-like aspect of Homo antecessor found at the neighbouring Gran Dolina site (900,000–800,000 years ago).
