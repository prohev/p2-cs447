Subject: A demo of Half-Life 2 RTX arrives next week on Steam
Date: Thu, 13 Mar 2025 12:19:08 -0500
Message-ID: <VeQBQO@www.engadget.com>
Author: Igor Bonifacic

<p>First announced <a data-i13n="cpos:1;pos:1" href="https://www.engadget.com/half-life-2-is-getting-an-unofficial-rtx-remaster-130006917.html">back in 2023</a>, NVIDIA is finally releasing a playable demo for<em> Half-Life 2 RTX</em>. If you own the original game on <a data-i13n="cpos:2;pos:1" href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/2477290/HalfLife_2_RTX/">Steam</a> (and if you don&#39;t, <a data-i13n="cpos:3;pos:1" href="https://www.engadget.com/gaming/pc/valve-celebrates-half-life-2s-20th-anniversary-with-a-big-update-174316547.html">what&#39;s your excuse</a>?), you can download the preview on March 18. The showcase will allow players to see how <a data-i13n="cpos:4;pos:1" href="https://www.hl2rtx.com/">Orbifold Studios</a> has reimagined Ravenholm and Nova Prospekt with ray tracing. NVIDIA says the full release will arrive &quot;at a later date.&quot;</p>
<div id="35b6b9866369412b99f02279cbd9a459"><div style="left:0;width:100%;height:0;position:relative;padding-bottom:56.25%;"><iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/j31ISEd8xRM?rel=0" style="top:0;left:0;width:100%;height:100%;position:absolute;border:0;" allowfullscreen="" scrolling="no" data-embed-domain="www.youtube.com"></iframe></div></div>
<p>Ahead of the demo&#39;s release, NVIDIA is also releasing <a data-i13n="cpos:5;pos:1" href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/rtx-ai-garage-rtx-remix/">RTX Remix</a>, the modding toolkit Orbifold used to remaster <em>HL2</em>, to the public. The suite is available to <a data-i13n="cpos:6;pos:1" href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce/rtx-remix/">download today</a>, and includes support for many of the company&#39;s latest technologies, including multi-frame generation via <a data-i13n="cpos:7;pos:1" href="https://www.engadget.com/computing/nvidia-announced-dlss-4-will-come-to-all-rtx-gpus-044835926.html">DLSS 4</a>. Additionally, NVIDIA says RTX Remix features an “easily mastered” interface that allows modders to add fully ray-traced lighting and AI-enhanced textures to older games without fuss. For more dedicated teams, RTX Remix makes it possible to rebuild every asset in a game.</p>
<span id="end-legacy-contents"></span><p>Beside the chance to see <em>Half-Life 2</em> in a whole new light, there&#39;s another good reason to revisit the game next week. Viktor Antonov, <em>Half-Life 2&#39;s </em>visionary art director, <a data-i13n="cpos:8;pos:1" href="https://www.engadget.com/gaming/viktor-antonov-art-director-for-half-life-2-and-dishonored-has-died-according-to-colleagues-203219167.html">passed away in February</a> at the age of 52. All video games are a collaborative effort, but if there&#39;s one person who helped make <em>Half-Life 2</em> so memorable, it&#39;s Antonov. He designed most of City 17 and the Combine technology that gives the game its unique visual identity.</p>
