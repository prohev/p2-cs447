<p><em>Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles: Tactical Takedown</em> will be released on <a data-i13n="cpos:1;pos:1" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xDby7SjDfRk"><ins>May 22 for PC via Steam</ins></a>. As the name indicates, this is a turn-based strategy RPG. It also happens to be the <a data-i13n="cpos:2;pos:1" href="https://www.engadget.com/gaming/pc/teenage-mutant-ninja-turtles-tactical-takedown-is-the-first-turn-based-game-in-the-franchises-long-history-175730833.html"><ins>first turn-based game in franchise history</ins></a>.</p>
<p>It’s being developed by Strange Scaffold, <a data-i13n="cpos:3;pos:1" href="https://www.engadget.com/even-the-indie-game-el-paso-elsewhere-is-getting-turned-into-a-movie-191423219.html"><ins>who made the fantastic </ins><em><ins>El Paso, Elsewhere</ins></em></a>. There’s a new trailer with plenty of gameplay, which is always nice. One thing that sets this tactical RPG apart from <a data-i13n="cpos:4;pos:1" href="https://www.engadget.com/unicorn-overlord-review-a-must-play-for-tactics-fans-173259209.html">rivals like <em>Unicorn Overlord</em> </a>is that the battles take just a few minutes to complete. As a matter of fact, they look even zippier than the streamlined fights found in the Mario + Rabbids series.</p>
<span id="end-legacy-contents"></span><div id="0f104aa9f8944697aa804534a7bb57de"><iframe width="560" height="315" src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/xDby7SjDfRk?si=RttSWaHBu7qhEmsV" title="YouTube video player" frameborder="0" allowfullscreen=""></iframe></div>
<p>The story sounds downright depressing for a TMNT game. “Splinter and Shredder are dead, and as the Turtles approach adulthood, they&#39;re not just growing up... they&#39;re growing apart,” a description reads. To that end, most of the levels are played solo with just a single turtle. Come on guys. Hash things out over a large pizza pie!</p>
<p>There’s no price yet, <a data-i13n="cpos:5;pos:1" href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/3229100/Teenage_Mutant_Ninja_Turtles_Tactical_Takedown/"><ins>but preorders are open</ins></a>. Publisher Paramount Game Studios has yet to say anything about a potential console release.</p>This article originally appeared on Engadget at https://www.engadget.com/gaming/pc/teenage-mutant-ninja-turtles-tactical-takedown-comes-out-on-may-22-191533579.html?src=rss
