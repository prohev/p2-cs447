The book is one insider’s look at Facebook/Meta, and how tech fat cats like Mark Zuckerberg and Sheryl Sandberg went off the rails. To quote the jacket copy, the book explores “how the more power they grasp, the less responsible they become.”</p>
<p>Naturally, Meta is pissed at Flatiron Books, and issued an arbitration order demanding that the publisher stop promoting or publishing the book.</p>
<p>But Flatiron is displaying some impressive backbone and is refusing to back down, according to tech outlet <a href="%5B%E2%80%98Careless%20People%E2%80%99%20publisher%20won%E2%80%99t%20pull%20the%20book%20Meta%20is%20trying%20to%20stop%5D%28https%3A//www.theverge.com/news/629347/meta-careless-people-flatiron-books-macmillan%29"><em>The Verge</em></a> and their own public statement:</p>
