<!-- SC_OFF --><div class="md"><p>In one of my projects, I heavily use underscores in identifiers. None of the crazy stuff like &quot;a leading underscore followed by a capital letter&quot;, that is so strongly reserved for the implementation. But many of my identifiers end with underscores or contain underscores in the middle.</p> <p>Seems like every developer has a different opinion about this, and in every discussion, the holy ANSI-C standard is cited:</p> <blockquote> <p>The use of two underscores (`__&#39;) in identifiers is reserved for the compiler&#39;s internal use according to the ANSI-C standard.</p> </blockquote> <p>However, ANSI-C defines also other restrictions, that seem a little bit outdated to me. In my project, I use C++20, soon switching to C++23; the code is C++-styled and not C-styled like in the glorious old days of programming...</p> <p>Just wanted to hear your thoughts about the underscore topic. Do you use it?
