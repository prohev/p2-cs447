Subject: Starfield On PS5 Still Not Confirmed But New Evidence Emerges

<p>Bethesda's 2023 RPG Starfield is not <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/starfield-on-ps5-isnt-ruled-out-xboxs-phil-spencer-says/1100-6529031/">ruled out for PS5</a>, but it also hasn't been announced for Sony's system like some of Bethesda's other games have, including <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/doom-the-dark-ages-release-date-trailer-story/1100-6526483/">Doom: The Dark Ages</a> and Indiana Jones and the Great Circle. Now, a listing on Bethesda's modding website might suggest a PS5 edition of the game is coming. </p><p><a href="https://www.reddit.com/r/Starfield/comments/1jb6ldu/they_have_added_the_playstation_logo_to_a/">Reddit user Dollar99Man</a> reshared a finding from YouTube user <a href="https://www.youtube.com/@510DeshawnPlays/videos">510deshawn</a> who discovered that the Bethesda Creation Kit website for Starfield was updated with the PlayStation logo, sitting alongside the logos for PC and Xbox. However, the Starfield Creation Kit hub <a href="https://creations.bethesda.net/en/starfield/all?sort=created_at&amp;timePeriod=ALL">does not allow players to choose "PlayStation" as a platform in the filter</a>, despite the logo appearing on one of the mod's pages.</p><p>It's possible to<a href="https://creations.bethesda.net/en/starfield/all?platforms=PS&amp;sort=created_at&amp;timePeriod=ALL"> tweak the URL</a> to force the filter to show mods listed for PlayStation. For a period of time today, March 14, this displayed one result for the mod listed for PlayStation. However, it has since been removed.</p><a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/starfield-on-ps5-still-not-confirmed-but-new-evidence-emerges/1100-6530141/?ftag=CAD-01-10abi2f/">Continue Reading at GameSpot</a>
