Author: Evan Campbell

<p dir="ltr">Square Enix has announced a crystal-shattering sales milestone for the Final Fantasy franchise. Worldwide, the RPG series has now topped 200 million copies sold, with the achievement apparently coinciding with the Final Fantasy Pixel Remaster series surpassing 5 million units sold.</p><p dir="ltr">The official Final Fantasy account <a href="https://x.com/FinalFantasy/status/1900396747647754617">on X</a> shared the Ifrit-sized accomplishment for the series. The post also thanks players for "wherever you joined us on our adventures." The RPG franchise began in 1987 with the original Final Fantasy, with the most recent mainline addition being <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/final-fantasy-16-review-on-its-own-terms/1900-6418081/">Final Fantasy XVI</a> in 2023. Last year saw the launch of <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/final-fantasy-7-rebirth-review-destinys-child/1900-6418187/">Final Fantasy VII Rebirth</a>, and the remake made its way to PC at the beginning of this year. In fact, the game starring Cloud Strife and crew <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/final-fantasy-7-rebirth-sets-a-new-record-for-the-longtime-series/1100-6529033/">set a Steam concurrent-players record for the series</a>. The PC release has also led to Final Fantasy VII Rebirth becoming <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/gallery/2025-best-selling-games/2900-6281/">one of 2025's best-selling games so far</a>. It's an intriguing development after Square Enix noted that <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/square-enix-says-final-fantasy-16-and-final-fantasy-7-rebirth-didnt-meet-sales-expectations/1100-6526552/">Final Fantasy VII Rebirth and Final Fantasy XVI failed to meet sales expectations</a> in September.</p><div data-embed-type="tweet" data-src="https://x.com/FinalFantasy/status/1900396747647754617" data-has-image="true"><blockquote align="center" class="twitter-tweet" data-conversation="none" data-mce-disable-toolbar="true"><p dir="ltr">The Final Fantasy Pixel Remaster series has sold over 5 million copies worldwide, and the Final Fantasy series has now sold over 200 million copies across the globe!<br />Wherever you joined us on our adventures, thank you. <a href="https://t.co/6eoIoDBXh0">pic.twitter.com/6eoIoDBXh0</a></p>  — FINAL FANTASY (@FinalFantasy) <a href="https://twitter.com/FinalFantasy/status/1900396747647754617?ref_src=twsrc^tfw">March 14, 2025</a></blockquote>          <text></text>    </div><p dir="ltr">The Final Fantasy Pixel Remaster series--consisting of the first six mainline games in the franchise--originally launched on PC and mobile devices in 2021. <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/final-fantasy-pixel-remaster-collection-is-coming-to-consoles-on-april-19/1100-6513014/">Square Enix then brought the Pixel Remaster series to Switch and PS4</a> in April 2023, with an Xbox Series X|S release happening in 2024. Speaking of Microsoft, <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/final-fantasy-xivs-full-xbox-release-date-confirmed-alongside-free-game-pass-expansions/1100-6521644/">Final Fantasy XIV finally arrived on Xbox Series X|S</a> last year, too. The MMO's success has actually <a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/square-enix-will-bring-more-games-to-xbox-after-final-fantasy-14s-success/1100-6526160/">led to Square Enix promising more of its games for Xbox</a>. </p><a href="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/cue-the-victory-fanfare-as-final-fantasy-franchise-surpasses-200-million-copies-sold/1100-6530133/?ftag=CAD-01-10abi2f/">Continue Reading at GameSpot</a>
